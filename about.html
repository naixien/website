About Naixien

An operating network for ambitious African businesses

Make it easier for African businesses to become ready for opportunity and easier for the right people, capital and organisations to find them.

Mission

Ready. Connected. Visible.

African businesses often lack easy access to the infrastructure, expertise and networks required to become investment-ready, acquisition-ready or strategically scalable. Those resources exist, but they are fragmented. Naixien brings them together around the business itself.

We give businesses useful reasons to join before asking them to pay: readiness scoring, free playbooks, and eventually a network of NEDs, partners and opportunities.

Who it is for

Primary and secondary audiences

  • African businesses: startups, SMEs, scale-ups, family-owned and growth companies
  • Investors and strategic buyers seeking African opportunities
  • Professionals: NEDs, executives, advisers, accountants and lawyers
  • Service providers offering cloud, software and professional support

Principle

Free value first

The early product deliberately provides substantial value without charge. Build a high-quality business community. Capture structured readiness insight. Facilitate relationships. Monetise the economic activity that happens within the network later.
