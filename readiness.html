How ready is your business?

Complete a structured assessment and receive an overall score plus dimension scores, strengths, gaps and recommended actions. Free at launch for the early cohort.

What you receive

A clear picture, then a path forward.

The score answers four questions: how ready you are, what to improve, who can help, and which opportunities may fit later.

  • Overall readiness score out of 100
  • Scores across eight business dimensions
  • Strengths and priority gaps
  • Recommended playbooks and resources
  • Pointers toward relevant people and partners

The Naixien Business Readiness Score is a diagnostic and educational tool. It is not a formal valuation, investment recommendation or professional certification.

Eight dimensions

What the score measures

Each dimension surfaces practical gaps African businesses often face before investment, partnership or acquisition conversations.

Financial

Reporting quality, visibility of performance, and readiness of financial information for diligence.

Governance

Board structures, oversight habits, policies and decision discipline that support sustainable growth.

Legal & corporate structure

Entity clarity, ownership documentation and legal foundations needed for partnerships and transactions.

Operations

Process maturity, delivery reliability and the ability to scale without constant founder intervention.

Technology

Systems, security posture and digital maturity that support resilience and future opportunity.

Management

Leadership depth, role clarity and the strength of the team around the founder.

Market position

Customer traction, competitive clarity and the story the market already knows about your business.

Transaction readiness

Preparation for investment, acquisition, partnership or other strategic conversations when the moment arrives.
